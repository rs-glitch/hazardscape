# CA Fire Years: charter (phase 0)

**Objective.** A lightweight browser app that replays how California's largest recent wildfires actually spread, on rotatable 3D terrain with vegetation, roads, towns and buildings, with stats that change in step with the fire line.

**Users.** Curious readers and the project owner; desktop and iPhone browsers.

Change from the first draft: the raw satellite area overstates small fires (Palisades +47 %) because 375 m pixels inflate edges. So the extent now comes from the official perimeter and the satellites set the timing. The raw bias is still reported in `data/validation.json`.

## Scope
- Fires (12): August Complex, Creek (2020); Dixie, Caldor, Monument (2021); Mosquito (2022); York (2023); Park, Bridge (2024); Palisades, Eaton, Gifford (2025).
- Statewide context: all CAL FIRE-listed incidents 2020–2026, WFIGS final perimeters > 1,000 ac.
- Out of scope: fire behaviour modelling, damage/fatality claims beyond published official fields, live data.

## Method
1. Spread: NASA FEDS 12-hourly perimeters (2020–2021 fires here; time label shifted +12 h, see README) where available; otherwise our reconstruction from VIIRS 375 m active-fire detections (S-NPP + NOAA-20): 12 h bins, cumulative union of pixel footprints, morphological close, hole fill. Every method is then clipped to the official final perimeter (valid geometry); official cells never flagged take the arrival time of the nearest burned cell.
2. Smooth fire-line motion: arrival time inside each 12 h step interpolated by distance between consecutive perimeters (labelled "interpolated" in the UI).
3. Terrain: Terrarium elevation tiles (USGS 3DEP/SRTM-derived). Vegetation: pre-fire NLCD land cover. Roads and towns: US Census TIGER (Overpass was overloaded). Buildings: OSM as of the day before ignition (Overpass attic query), small fires only.
4. Stats per step: burned area, growth, active detections, FRP, burned land cover by class, OSM buildings inside the line, hourly weather (ERA5 via Open-Meteo).
